Welcome to ReLeet

When you solve a problem on LeetCode, ReLeet asks how hard it felt and books time in your calendar to review it again, a few days later or a few months, depending on your answer.

ReLeet is already active on another computer, so this one stays idle.

The other computer stops checking LeetCode and updating your calendars. Your problems stay there: export them in its Settings and import them here.

  1. Sign in to LeetCode

    ReLeet finds out who you are from leetcode.com, so it can notice your solves.

    Open LeetCode

    Waiting for you to sign in…

  2. Import your recent solves (up to 20)

    Your latest accepted problems go to the ReLeet popup, for you to rate. You can skip this.

  3. Connect a calendar (optional)

    ReLeet makes its own calendar named “ReLeet” and books each review there. Without one, the ReLeet popup still shows what's due.

  4. When to review

    minutes a day

Everything stays in this browser. You can change all of this later in Settings.
