ReLeet settings

Changes save as you make them.

ReLeet is active on another computer, so this one doesn't check LeetCode or update calendars. See This computer.

LeetCode

When a submission on leetcode.com is accepted, ReLeet asks right there how hard it felt. It also checks your recent accepted submissions every 15 minutes while Chrome is open, which catches solves made on other devices.

Import recent solves adds your 20 most recent accepted problems that ReLeet doesn't track yet to the popup, for you to rate.

Schedule

minutes a day

Reviews that don't fit move to the next day with room. A day always gets at least one review.

Time for one review, by its last rating

Calendar reminder:

Reviews on the same day follow each other from the start time, weekends included. A missed review is booked again, first in line on the next day with room. Changes here move upcoming reviews in your calendars; ones that already started stay where they are.

Calendars

Each connected account gets its own calendar named “ReLeet”, with time booked for every review and a reminder before it. Rating a problem moves its review; stopping tracking deletes it.

Without a calendar, the popup still shows when each review is due, and its ⋯ menu can add a review to any calendar app.

Notifications

One notification at your start time that says how many reviews are due that day. Solves ReLeet notices in the background still get their own notification.

This computer

The other computer stops checking LeetCode and updating your calendars. Your problems stay there: export them on that computer and import them here.

Move ReLeet to another computer

Export saves your problems, settings and calendar details to a file. Import it on the other computer, then connect your calendars there again: ReLeet keeps using the same “ReLeet” calendar. The file holds no passwords or sign-ins.

Privacy & data

  • Your problems, ratings and settings stay in this browser. ReLeet has no server, sends nothing to its developer, and has no analytics.
  • ReLeet reads your recent accepted solves from leetcode.com, using your public profile.
  • If you connect Google Calendar or Apple Calendar, ReLeet writes your reviews there: each one has the problem's name and link.
  • Your Apple app-specific password is stored encrypted in this browser. Google sign-in is handled by Chrome, and ReLeet never sees your Google password.
